Frequently Asked Interview Questions

Tell me about yourself.

Give a short summary of your education, key skills, and what you're looking for in this role. Keep it under 1 minute.

What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Pick a real strength relevant to the job, and a genuine weakness you're actively improving.

Why should we hire you?

Connect your skills directly to what the role needs. Be specific, not generic.
